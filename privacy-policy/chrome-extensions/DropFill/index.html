DropFill

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

In short: your data never leaves your computer. DropFill reads your images, PDFs and files locally, keeps your data blocks in your own browser, and has no analytics, tracking or advertising.

DropFill is a Chrome extension that reads images, PDFs, JSON, CSV and text files on your computer and lets you drag the extracted data into web forms. This policy explains what happens to your data.

What DropFill processes

  • Files, screenshots and text you give it (images, PDFs, JSON, CSV, TXT, pasted text, captures of the current tab). They are processed entirely inside your browser: text recognition (OCR) runs locally with Tesseract.js (WebAssembly) and PDFs are read locally with pdf.js.
  • Form fields you drop data on. The content script only acts when you drop a DropFill block onto a field; it writes the value into that field and nothing else. While the side panel is closed it shows a small "DropFill" tab on the page edge to reopen it. It does not read, record or send the content of the pages you visit.

What DropFill stores, and where

All of the following stays on your computer or in your own Chrome profile:

DataWhereWhy
Extracted text and data blockschrome.storage.localSo the panel keeps your data when you close and reopen it. Delete it any time with "Clear all".
Source images (to select a region later)The extension's IndexedDBDeleted together with their group.
Downloaded OCR language dataThe extension's IndexedDBSo languages work offline. Delete them in Settings.
Settings (drop behaviour, separator, date/number format, OCR languages, reminder on/off)chrome.storage.syncChrome syncs them between your own signed-in browsers.
Number of successful fillschrome.storage.localOnly used to show an occasional donation reminder. Never sent anywhere.

Uninstalling the extension removes all of it.

Network requests

DropFill makes exactly one kind of network request: when you choose a new OCR language, it downloads that language's data file (for example vie.traineddata.gz) from the public jsDelivr CDN (cdn.jsdelivr.net, package @tesseract.js-data). This is a plain file download; no images, documents, text or personal data are sent. English is bundled with the extension and needs no download.

DropFill contains no analytics, no tracking, no advertising and no remote code.

Clipboard

DropFill writes to the clipboard only when you click a data block or its copy button, or when a value could not be put into a field, so that you can paste it yourself. DropFill never reads your clipboard.

Donations

Donations are voluntary and handled entirely by the payment service you choose (PayPal or MoMo) in a normal browser tab. DropFill does not know who donated, collects no email address and does not track clicks on donation links. Donating does not unlock any feature.

Permissions

  • sidePanel – show the DropFill panel next to the page.
  • storage – keep your data blocks and settings (see the table above).
  • clipboardWrite – copy a data block when you click it, and copy a value DropFill could not put into a field so you can paste it yourself.
  • scripting – after installation, add the drop handler to tabs that were already open, so you do not have to reload them.
  • Access to all sites (<all_urls>) – so you can drop data into forms on any website (including inside frames) and capture the visible tab when you click "Capture this tab".

Children

DropFill is a general productivity tool and does not knowingly collect any data from anyone.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at the same address with a new date. For questions, use the contact email shown on DropFill's Chrome Web Store page.

DropFill is free. Optional support: the donation page inside the extension (PayPal or MoMo).
