Privacy Policy — Toy Block Takedown

Effective 20 October 2026

Toy Block Takedown ("the game") is published by hudacode ("we"). This policy explains what information the game handles. The short version: your progress stays on your device, and the game shows ads from Google AdMob, which collects some device information to serve them. We have no accounts and no servers of our own, and we never see or store personal information.

Information the game stores on your device

The game saves your progress (current stack, score, best stars, boosters left, the tiles left on the board for the next stack) and your settings (sound, vibration, whether you have seen the tutorial). All of it stays in the game's private storage on your device. It is never uploaded, and it is removed when you uninstall the game.

Advertising

The game is free and shows ads: a banner at the bottom of the screen, full-screen ads at breaks in play and when you come back to the game, ads on the pause and game-over screens, and optional video ads you can choose to watch for a free booster.

The ads are provided by Google AdMob. To show ads, measure them, limit how often you see the same ad and prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK in the game collects and sends to Google:

  • your device's advertising ID and other device or app identifiers,
  • your IP address, which may be used to estimate your general location (country or city, not your precise location),
  • information about your device and the game (model, operating system, language, app version),
  • how you interact with ads (views, taps, video plays),
  • diagnostic data such as crash logs and performance information.

This information goes directly from your device to Google; we do not receive or store it. Google may use it to show personalised ads, based on your interests, where the law allows and you have agreed. Google handles it under its own policies:

  • How Google uses information from apps that use its services: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
  • Google Privacy Policy: policies.google.com/privacy

Your choices

  • Consent (EEA, UK and Switzerland). If you are in one of these regions, the game asks for your consent through Google's consent form before personalised ads are shown. You can refuse; you may then see fewer or non-personalised ads.
  • Personalised ads. On Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your device settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads, depending on the device).
  • Playing offline. The game works without an internet connection; no ads are loaded and no data is sent while you are offline.

What the game does not do

  • It has no accounts, no sign-in and no servers of its own, and contains no analytics software of its own.
  • It does not ask for or collect your name, email address, contacts, photos, precise location or payment details, and has no in-app purchases.
  • Permissions: vibration (haptic feedback while you play), plus internet access, network state and the advertising ID, which the ads SDK needs to load ads.

Children
