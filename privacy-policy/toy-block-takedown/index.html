Privacy Policy — Toy Block Takedown

Effective 26 September 2026

Toy Block Takedown ("the game") is published by hudacode ("we"). This policy explains what

information the game handles. The short version: the game does not collect, share or send

any personal information. Everything it stores stays on your device.

Information the game stores on your device

The game saves your progress (current level, best stars, boosters left, the tiles left on the

board for the next level) and your settings (sound, vibration, whether you have seen the

tutorial). All of it stays in the game's private storage on your device. It is never uploaded,

and it is removed when you uninstall the game.

What the game does not do

  • It shows no ads and contains no advertising or analytics software.
  • It has no accounts, no sign-in and no online features; it works fully offline.
  • It does not collect your name, email address, contacts, photos, location, device

    • identifiers, payment details or any other personal information.

  • The only permission it asks for is vibration, used for haptic feedback while you play.

Children

The game has no personal data collection of any kind, so it collects nothing from children

either.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at the same address with a new

effective date. If a future version adds ads or analytics, this policy will be updated before

that version is released.
