Privacy Policy — Pickup Pals

Effective 20 October 2026

Pickup Pals ("the game") is published by hudacode ("we"). This policy explains what information the game handles. The short version: the game does not collect, send or share any personal information. Your progress stays on your device.

Information the game stores on your device

The game saves your progress (levels unlocked and completed, coins, boosters left) and your settings (sound, vibration). All of it stays in the game's private storage on your device. It is never uploaded, and it is removed when you uninstall the game or clear its data.

What the game does not do

  • It has no accounts, no sign-in and no servers of its own.
  • It contains no advertising and no analytics or tracking software.
  • It has no in-app purchases. Coins are earned only by playing and cannot be bought.
  • It does not ask for or collect your name, email address, contacts, photos, location, device identifiers or payment details.
  • It works fully offline and sends no data over the internet.
  • Permissions: vibration, for haptic feedback while you play.

Children

The game does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, for example because a later version adds features that handle data, the new version will be published at the same address with a new effective date before that version is released.
