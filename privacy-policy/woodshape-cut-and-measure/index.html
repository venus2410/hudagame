Privacy Policy — WoodShape: Cut & Measure

Effective 16 September 2026

WoodShape: Cut & Measure ("the game") is published by hudacode ("we"). This policy explains what information the game handles and why. The short version: the game keeps your progress on your own device and does not collect personal information itself; the ads it shows are served by Google AdMob, which processes some device data as described below.

Information the game stores on your device

The game saves your progress (current order, best stars, experience and trade rank, hints left), benches you save mid-order, pictures you save to the gallery and layout preferences. All of it stays in the game's private storage on your device. It is not uploaded anywhere, and it is removed when you uninstall the game. The game does not ask for any device permissions (no location, camera, microphone, contacts or file access).

Advertising

The game shows ads through Google AdMob (the Google Mobile Ads SDK). To serve and measure ads, Google may collect and process:

  • your device's advertising ID and similar device identifiers;
  • an approximate location derived from your IP address;
  • information about the ads you see and interact with;
  • diagnostic and performance data about the ads SDK.

How Google uses this data is described in Google's Privacy Policy and in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom), the game asks for your consent before showing personalised ads, using Google's User Messaging Platform. You can change your choice later from the game's ad-consent form, and you can reset or delete your advertising ID at any time in your device's settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads).

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Ads are requested with Google's standard (non-child-directed) settings.

Data we never collect

We do not collect your name, email address, contacts, photos, precise location, payment details or any account information. The game has no accounts and no online features.

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at the same address with a new effective date.
